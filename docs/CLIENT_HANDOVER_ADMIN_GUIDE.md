## 1. Accessing the Admin Dashboard

1. Navigate to: **`https://talenthubconsultant.com/admin`**
2. Enter your authorized administrator email and password.
3. Click the **Eye Icon** inside the password field if you want to verify your entered password.
4. Click **Sign In**.

### If you forget your password:
1. Click **Forgot password?** on the login screen.
2. Enter your registered email address and submit.
3. Check your inbox for a secure recovery link. The link contains a one-time cryptographic token that expires in 60 minutes.
4. Click the link, choose your new password (minimum 8 characters, with letters and numbers), and sign in.

## 2. Managing Job Openings

**Navigation:** *Sidebar → Recruitment & Hiring → Job Openings*

* **Create a New Job:**
  1. Click **+ Post New Job** at the top right.
  2. Fill in Job Title, Department/Category, Employment Type (Full Time, Contract, etc.), Experience Level, and Location.
  3. Enter the Job Description and Key Responsibilities using the built-in rich text editor.
  4. Select Status: **Published** to make it live immediately on the website, or **Draft** to review later.
  5. Click **Publish Job**.

* **Edit or Close an Existing Job:**
  1. Find the job in the table.
  2. Click the **Pencil (Edit)** icon to modify requirements or title.
  3. To pause applications without deleting, change status to **Closed** or click the **Deactivate** toggle.
  4. Use the **Eye Icon** to preview the exact details.

## 3. Reviewing Applications & Candidate Vault

**Navigation:** *Sidebar → Recruitment & Hiring → Job Applications* or *Candidate Vault*

* **Filter Applications:** Use the filters at the top to filter by Job Title, Candidate Status (*New, Screening, Interviewed, Offered, Rejected*), or Date Applied.
* **Review Candidate Details:** Click on any candidate name to view contact numbers, email, current company, notice period, and expected CTC.
* **Download Resumes:** Click the **Download Resume** button to safely inspect the candidate's PDF or Word CV from the secure document vault.
* **Update Status:** Change candidate status to trigger automated internal workflow tracking.

## 4. General Resume Bank & Talent Pool

**Navigation:** *Sidebar → Recruitment & Hiring → Resume Drop Bank*

* Candidates who submit resumes through the website's general "Drop Your Resume" form appear here.
* Search candidates by keywords, skills, or experience.
* Convert a general candidate into an active applicant for an open mandate with one click.

## 5. Employer CRM & Corporate Inquiries

**Navigation:** *Sidebar → Recruitment & Hiring → Client Companies* and *Sidebar → Inquiries & Follow-ups*

* **Corporate Talent Requisitions:** When companies submit hiring requirements via the website, they are automatically logged into the Inquiries queue.
* **Assign Follow-up:** Track follow-up dates, notes, and assign recruitment leads to team members.
* **Client Companies:** Manage registered client employers, active mandate counts, and primary HR contacts.

## 6. Updating Website Content & FAQs (CMS)

**Navigation:** *Sidebar → Website Content & SEO → Page Layouts & Text* and *Blogs, FAQs & Reviews*

* **Edit Page Content:** Select any public page (*About Us, Solutions, Employers, Candidates, Contact*) and update banner text, mission statements, or statistical numbers.
* **Add Industry Insights / Blog Articles:** Create articles to boost Google ranking. Add featured images, categories, and full formatting.
* **Manage FAQs:** Add or update commonly asked questions to help job seekers and employers understand your recruitment process.
* **Media Library:** Upload corporate pictures, logos, and recruitment marketing assets directly.

## 7. Website Control Center & Emergency Maintenance

**Navigation:** *Sidebar → Administration & System → Website Control & Maintenance*

This is your central command center to control platform availability without needing developer assistance:

* **Activate Maintenance Mode:**
  1. Click the **Activate Maintenance** toggle.
  2. Customize the public announcement headline, explanation message, and expected return time.
  3. Click **Preview Page** to see how public visitors will see the HTTP 503 screen.
  4. Click **Save All Controls**.
  *Note:* Administrators can still access the dashboard at all times even when maintenance mode is active.

* **Module Switches:**
  * Toggle **Job Openings** on/off.
  * Toggle **Job Applications** on/off.
  * Toggle **General Resume Uploads** on/off.
  * Toggle **Employer Inquiries** on/off.
  * Toggle **General Contact Form** on/off.
  *All switches are strictly enforced by the backend.*
