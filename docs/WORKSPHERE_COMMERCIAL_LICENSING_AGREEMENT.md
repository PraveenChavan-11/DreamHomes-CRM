## 1. Executive Summary

WorkSphere is an enterprise-grade multi-tenant HRMS, Indian statutory payroll, and workforce operating platform engineered by **Praveen Chavan** and TalentHub.

To serve both operational end-users and technology partners, WorkSphere is offered under a dual-model commercial framework:

1. **Subscription (Software as a Service — SaaS):** For employers, HR teams, and business enterprises seeking a turnkey, fully managed cloud service with zero DevOps or maintenance responsibilities.
2. **Licence with Source Code:** For IT companies, software consultancies, managed service providers (MSPs), and systems integrators seeking to own the product, host on their own infrastructure, white-label, customize, extend, and offer solutions to their commercial client base.

## 3. Model 1: Hosted Subscription (SaaS)

### 3.1. Overview
Your organization uses WorkSphere as a managed cloud service. TalentHub provisions a secure, isolated workspace and tenant database. You pay a transparent subscription based on the chosen feature plan and active employee headcount, billed monthly, quarterly, or yearly.

### 3.2. What Is Included
- **Turnkey Cloud Deployment:** Zero server administration, database tuning, or security patching required from your team.
- **Automated Compliance:** Routine statutory updates (PF, ESIC, Professional Tax, TDS formulas) and continuous system improvements deployed automatically.
- **Data Privacy & Security:** Strict tenant isolation preventing cross-organization data leakage, coupled with encrypted credential handling and daily automated backups.
- **Risk-Free Onboarding:** Begin with a 7-day sandbox demo workspace, transition into a live evaluation period, and activate a paid subscription only when fully satisfied.

### 3.3. Source Code Exclusion
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Organisations adopting WorkSphere via Subscription receive full usage rights to the cloud service. **A subscription does not include or convey rights to the source code.** Source code is provided solely under Model 2 with a formal licensing agreement.

## 4. Model 2: Licence with Source Code (For IT & Software Companies)

### 4.1. Overview
A technology company wishing to own the product can license WorkSphere together with its source code. What is handed over, the commercial terms, and the support period are agreed in writing with Praveen Chavan / TalentHub before anything is delivered.

### 4.2. The 7 Discussion Pillars for IT Companies
Every source code licensing discussion between TalentHub and an IT partner covers seven key pillars:

1. **Rights & Permitted Limits:** Clear contractual definition of your rights, permitted deployment volume, multi-tenant hosting boundaries, and client resale scope.
2. **Source Code & Documentation Handover:** Delivery of the complete modern PHP 8.2 codebase, CodeIgniter 4 framework extensions, MariaDB/MySQL database schemas, migrations, and developer architecture documentation.
3. **Deployment & Knowledge-Transfer Support:** Setup advisory, infrastructure sizing, continuous integration guidance, and engineering knowledge transfer to your developers.
4. **Branding & White-Labelling:** Rights to modify stylesheets, replace marks and brand badges, bind custom domains, and skin client-facing portals under your corporate identity.
5. **Customisation Built to Order:** Scoping and engineering of bespoke modules, proprietary third-party integrations, or industry-specific workflow extensions built by TalentHub.
6. **Support & Maintenance Period:** Dedicated post-delivery warranty covering technical bug fixes, security patches, framework updates, and engineering advisory.
7. **Price & Payment Milestones:** Transparent commercial terms structured into milestone-based payments tied to technical deliverables and mutual sign-offs.

> [!NOTE]
> These are discussion points, not a fixed price list. Each licence is scoped individually based on your intended usage, deployment scale, and engineering support requirements, with all commitments formalized in a binding written agreement.
